2. What Allkept is

Allkept collects things you have saved elsewhere into one private library, sorts them into categories automatically, and lets you search them.

Saves reach Allkept three ways:

  • you send a post, reel or link to @allkeptapp on Instagram;
  • you connect a YouTube playlist of your own and add videos to it; or
  • you paste a link into the app, or share one into it from another app.

You can also bring in older Instagram saves by uploading the "saved posts" export that Meta gives you.

Allkept stores links, the public preview details of those links, a copy of the preview image, and whatever you write yourself. It does not download, copy, host or re-publish videos, audio or full posts. Playback happens through the platform's own embed, and every card links back to the original.

Notifications

Allkept can tell you when a save has landed or when one needs something from you. Notifications are service messages about your own library, not marketing. They are off until you turn them on, you can turn them off again at any time in Settings, and your phone's own settings override ours. A notification names the save it is about, so it may show a save's title on your lock screen — the Privacy Policy explains what that involves.

Automatic sorting can be turned off

Sorting saves into categories is on by default and can be switched off in Settings, in which case saves arrive uncategorised. Search indexing is a separate thing and stays on; see the Privacy Policy for exactly what that means.

5. Connecting Instagram, YouTube and imports

Instagram

You connect Instagram by sending a six-character code to @allkeptapp. That code links whichever Instagram account sent it to whichever Allkept account requested it — so do not give your code to anyone else, and do not send someone else's code. A code expires after ten minutes and can be used once.

We reply in that thread to confirm a save. You can stop those replies at any time by sending stop replies, or from Settings. We only ever message people who have messaged us first, and we never post anything on your behalf.

Some Instagram post shares arrive without the original link because Instagram does not include one. Those saves stay incomplete until you supply the link. That is a platform limitation, not a fault we can fix.

YouTube

You give us the link to a playlist. We read it periodically with our own YouTube API key and turn new videos in it into saves. We do not ask for, receive or store your Google or YouTube account credentials, and we can see nothing on YouTube except that one playlist. The playlist must be public or unlisted; YouTube shows Watch Later and Liked videos to nobody, including us. Disconnect it at any time in Settings.

Importing your older saves

You may upload the "saved posts" export Meta gives you, so that your existing Instagram saves appear in Allkept. Upload only an export of your own account. We read the Instagram post links out of it and delete the file immediately afterwards.

9. Automatic sorting and search

Allkept sorts saves into categories, writes a one-line summary and extracts tags automatically, by sending the text of the save to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI — see the Privacy Policy). Search combines exact matching with meaning-based matching, which sends the words you type to the same provider.

These are automated guesses, not statements of fact. A category can be wrong, a summary can be misleading, and a search can miss something. Do not rely on them for anything that matters. You can change any category yourself, and your correction is never overwritten.

Sorting is not a decision about you and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.
