# Category marks

The category marks in `lib/mark-svgs.ts` are from **Streamline Plump Flat**, the free icons at
https://streamlinehq.com, © Streamline (Webalys), used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They were taken from the Iconify distribution of the free set (`streamline-plump-color`, the
`-flat` icons) and changed only in colour: the set's two blues are replaced at draw time by the
category's own pair. `scripts/marks.mjs` regenerates the file from the key list in
`lib/category-marks.ts`.

The app credits Streamline in Settings, as the licence asks.
