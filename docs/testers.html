Testing Allkept

Thank you for trying this. Allkept collects the things you save on social platforms into one place, sorted and searchable. This test covers Instagram only, and should take about a minute to set up.

Install

  1. iPhone: accept the TestFlight invitation sent to your email, install TestFlight, then install Allkept from it.
  2. Android: open the Play link you were sent, join the test, then install Allkept from the Play Store.

Connect Instagram

  1. Open Allkept and tap Connect Instagram. It shows a six-character code.
  2. Tap Open Instagram. It opens a message to @allkeptapp.
  3. Send the code as a message. Allkept replies “Linked as @you”, and the app moves on by itself.

Save something

  1. In Instagram, tap the paper plane under any reel or post.
  2. Choose @allkeptapp and send.
  3. Within a few seconds you get a reply saying what it was filed under, and the card appears in Allkept.

Plain photo posts arrive without a link, because Instagram does not send one. Open that card in Allkept and paste the post's link to fill in the preview.

What we would like to know

  • Did you keep using it after the first day, and what made you stop?
  • Were the categories right? Correct any that were wrong; that is the most useful signal we get.
  • Anything that felt slow, confusing or broken.

Send feedback as a message to @allkeptapp on Instagram.
