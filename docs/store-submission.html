8. Review notes (drafts)

App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes

Allkept is a personal library for the things you save on social platforms. Sign-in is required because the library syncs to the account; there is no password login.

SIGN IN: Sign in with Apple works with any Apple ID. A Google test account is also provided in the credentials fields above.

TO SEE IT WORK (no Instagram needed):
1. Complete the short profile step (name and a photo).
2. Tap the + button, paste any public link (for example a YouTube video), and tap Save. A card appears with a preview and a category within a few seconds.
3. Search by any word in the title, or filter by topic.
4. Open the original from the card, or share it on.

Sending posts to @allkeptapp from Instagram requires linking an Instagram account; the paste-a-link path exercises the same pipeline.

Notifications are requested only when the user turns a switch on in Settings, never at launch.
Account deletion: Settings → "Delete account and everything in it".

Play Console → App access → Instructions

Sign in with the Google account provided. Complete the profile step (name and a photo). Tap the + button, paste any public link and tap Save; a card with a preview and a category appears within a few seconds. Search and filters are on the Library tab. Account deletion is in Settings.

Line 4 of the Apple notes is deliberately vague about Meta. See risk 1 before deciding how much to say. DECIDE

11. Risks worth knowing before you submit

  1. The Instagram door will not complete for a reviewer. RISK Until Meta approves the app (~20 days from today's submission), DMs to @allkeptapp only work for accounts added as Meta testers. If an Apple or Google reviewer taps "Connect Instagram" and never gets the "Linked as @you" reply, it looks broken (Apple guideline 2.1). Options: (a) submit the stores after Meta approves; (b) submit now with notes steering reviewers to paste-a-link and make sure the connect screen shows a calm "waiting for Instagram" state rather than a spinner forever — VERIFY what the screen does on no reply; (c) both. DECIDE
  2. Profile photo — was required at onboarding; optional and moved to Settings on 11 Sep. Apple 5.1.1(ii) question closed.
  3. Phone number — removed 11 Sep; stored values nulled. Closed.
  4. Policy pages — presentation fixed 11 Sep (task A). Whether to launch without a lawyer's pass remains your call; the pages no longer say so out loud.
  5. Google sign-in for Apple reviewers. Google's device challenge often blocks reviewers; Sign in with Apple is the reliable path and the notes say so.
  6. Support URL. Apple will not accept the testers page. Needs the support page.
  7. Brand names in keywords. Usually tolerated; occasionally flagged. §7.
  8. Manual first Android upload. Plan an hour in the Play Console the first time; the App content declarations gate the production release.
