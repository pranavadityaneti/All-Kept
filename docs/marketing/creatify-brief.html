1. Product context — paste into Creatify's "product description"

Creatify writes its own script from this field if you let it. Give it the truth, tightly, so anything it improvises stays inside what the app actually does.

All Kept is a mobile app (iOS and Android, launching soon — waitlist open now at allkept.app) that keeps everything you save on social media in one searchable library.

The problem: people save hundreds of reels, TikToks, YouTube videos and posts — recipes, places, outfits, workouts, tips — and can never find them again. Saved tabs have no search and no organisation. Saves pile up and get lost.

What All Kept does: you share a post or paste a link from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Reddit into All Kept. AI reads it and files it under a category — Food, Travel, Fitness, Design, Tech, Entertainment, Wellness and more (you can also create your own categories). Everything sits in one library with filters by platform and category, and one search bar that finds a save by what it's about.

Key messages:
- Everything you save, in one library.
- Sorted by AI. Found in one search.
- Works across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit.
- Coming to iOS and Android. Join the waitlist — one email when it opens, nothing else.

Do NOT claim: automatic capture (the user shares or pastes each save), Pinterest / X / WhatsApp support, that the app is available in the App Store or Play Store today, any pricing, any user numbers.

Tone: honest, a little wry, spoken like a friend who found a fix — not a launch-hype voice. Headline of the campaign: "2,000 saves. Good luck finding one."
In plain English: this paragraph is the leash. Creatify's AI will fill gaps with plausible-sounding features if you don't tell it what the app can't do. The "Do NOT claim" line matters more than the rest.

4. Rules every video must obey

  • No App Store / Google Play badges. The app isn't listed yet; both badge programmes prohibit it (and Meta reads it as a broken promise).
  • No fake UI. No drawn email fields, no fake "Download" buttons. Meta rejects non-functional features. The CTA is text: Join the waitlist · allkept.app.
  • Platforms named on screen or in voice: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit. Never Pinterest, X, WhatsApp.
  • Never say "automatically captures" or "syncs your saves". The user shares or pastes each save. Say "share it" / "paste the link" / "sorted by AI".
  • The end card is the ask, not "coming soon". "Coming soon" may appear as a small line; the big text is Join the waitlist.
  • No third-party creators' faces where a crop can avoid it. Film stills in the library are the riskiest — crop them out.
  • No invented numbers about the product ("10,000 users", "4.9 stars"). The only number allowed is the hyperbolic one addressed to the viewer: "2,000 saves".
  • AI-presenter disclosure: TikTok requires AI-generated content to be labelled (there's a toggle at upload). Meta doesn't require it for a product ad today, as far as I know — I'm not certain, so tick the disclosure if the option is offered; it costs nothing.
  • US spelling in US scripts ("favorites"), Indian-English in India scripts is fine but keep it neutral.

8. QA before upload — watch each render once, check:

  • Hook is audible and captioned by second 2. If the avatar does a "Hey guys" preamble, cut it.
  • No store badges, no fake buttons, no Pinterest/X/WhatsApp icons crept in from Creatify's library.
  • The voice never says "automatically", "syncs", "imports your saves".
  • Screenshots are ours, not stock; the "Explore interests" chip row and film-still cards are cropped out.
  • End card reads Join the waitlist · allkept.app, not "Download now".
  • US render uses the US avatar and voice; India render the Indian one.
  • Captions have no typos (Creatify's ASR sometimes hears "All Kept" as "all capped" — check every instance).
  • Export 1080×1920, H.264, ≤ 30 s, under 4 GB (Meta) — Creatify's default is fine.
In plain English: six short scripts, each built as pain → what the app does → the ask. Paste the product description so the AI doesn't make things up, paste our scripts instead of letting it write them, use only our screenshots, and make sure the last thing on screen is "Join the waitlist" — not an app-store badge for an app that isn't in the store yet.
