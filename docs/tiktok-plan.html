1. The short version

TikTok is not a new door to build — it is a door that is three-quarters built and never advertised. The system already recognises TikTok links (long, short and photo posts), strips their tracking keys, expands short links, files them as short videos or images, and fetches title, author and picture from TikTok's public oEmbed endpoint. The logo is in the app and the welcome screen already promises TikTok.

What is missing is what makes it feel integrated: playing the video inside Allkept (today only Instagram and YouTube play in place), bringing in a person's existing TikTok favourites from their data export (the way Instagram history comes in), and an end-to-end proof that a link shared from the TikTok app arrives as a full card. Roughly a week of focused work across three phases, none of which needs a TikTok developer account.

What is not possible: a "Connect TikTok" door that reads a person's favourites live. TikTok publishes no API for favourites or saved videos to third parties; its Login Kit and Display API expose only a person's own posted videos, which is not what Allkept is for.

4. The plan, in phases

Phase 1 — prove and polish the door that exists (about a day)

  • End-to-end check with real links: a long video link, a vm.tiktok.com share, a photo post, a profile, a region-locked or deleted video. Each must arrive as a card with title, author, picture and the right kind — or a calm "preview unavailable", never a wrong page (the isWrongPage guard in enrich.ts already exists for this).
  • Fix whatever that turns up (the oEmbed response for photo posts is the likely surprise).
  • Filter and library copy: make sure "TikTok" appears as a platform pill wherever Instagram and YouTube do.

Phase 2 — play inside Allkept (two to three days)

  • Add TikTok to embed.ts: build the embed page URL from the external id, the way the Instagram branch does; give EmbedPlayer the same tap-to-play and "keep the tap inside the card" treatment it gives Instagram (TikTok's embed is also eager to open new windows).
  • Verify on both platforms with the mobile preset: autoplay/inline behaviour, the aspect ratio (9:16, so the "video in a box of its own shape" fix from 10 Sep applies), and that a removed video degrades to the card rather than an error frame.
  • Photo posts: TikTok's photo-mode embed behaves differently; decide whether to embed or show the snapshot.

Phase 3 — bring in existing favourites (three to four days)

  • Extend the importer to recognise TikTok's export: a JSON (or the zip that holds it) with the favourite-videos list, and optionally the like list — same size limits and the same "wrong file" refusals as the Instagram import.
  • Normalise each entry through the existing TikTok normaliser; dedupe against the library; run the cheaper classifier tier already used for imported back catalogues.
  • Copy on the "Bring in your older saves" screen to explain where the TikTok export lives and that it can take TikTok a day or two to produce it.

What this does not include

  • A TikTok developer account, Login Kit or any TikTok API — none is needed for the three phases, and none would add a door that matters.
  • Downloading or storing the videos themselves. Allkept keeps the link, the caption and a snapshot of the picture, as it does for every platform.

5. Platform terms and the US situation

  • oEmbed and embed pages are TikTok's public tooling for third parties and carry no API agreement. Using them as designed — fetch the metadata for a link the person chose, show the official embed — is the conservative path. The [UNVERIFIED] item to close in the platform-terms audit (legal plan §2): whether TikTok's Terms restrict storing the thumbnail snapshot; every other platform's snapshot sits on the same reasoning, so it belongs in the same review.
  • No scraping. Everything above stays on public, documented endpoints. If oEmbed withholds something for a post, the answer is "preview unavailable", not a page fetch.
  • TikTok's US corporate arrangement. As of mid-2026 TikTok's US operations sit in a US joint venture under the 2025 divest-or-ban settlement. I am not certain how, or whether, that changes the public oEmbed/embed endpoints for US users; Phase 1's end-to-end check should be run on a US-region device or network before launch. VERIFY
  • Brand use. The logo comes from packages/platform-assets ("official platform artwork", 9 Sep). TikTok's brand guidelines allow the logo to indicate the platform a link comes from; naming TikTok in store listings is the same trademark question as Instagram and YouTube (worksheet §7).
