3. What Allkept is — and what it is not

3.1 What it does

Allkept gathers things you have saved elsewhere into one private library, sorts them into categories automatically, and lets you search them. Saves reach Allkept in the ways described in section 5.

For each save, Allkept stores the link, the public preview details of the link (title, caption or description, author name and handle), a copy of the preview image (a "thumbnail"), and whatever you write yourself. It does not download, copy, host or re-publish videos, audio or full posts. Playback happens through the platform's own embedded player, and every card links back to the original.

3.2 Automatic sorting and search

Allkept sorts saves into categories, writes a one-line summary and extracts tags automatically. To do that, it sends the text of the save to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenAI). Search combines exact matching with meaning-based matching, which sends the words you type to the same provider. The Privacy Policy describes exactly what is sent.

These are automated guesses, not statements of fact. A category can be wrong, a summary can be misleading, and a search can miss something. Do not rely on them for anything that matters. You can change any category yourself, and your correction is never overwritten.

Sorting is on by default and can be switched off in Settings; saves then arrive uncategorised. Search indexing is separate and stays on. Sorting is not a decision about you and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

3.3 Notifications

Allkept can tell you when a save has landed or when one needs something from you. Notifications are service messages about your own library, not marketing. They are off until you turn them on in Settings, you can turn them off again at any time, and your phone's own settings override ours.

3.4 What Allkept is not

  • Not a backup service. Platforms remove posts, change links and block previews. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.
  • Not a licence to other people's content. Saving something in Allkept gives you no right to use it beyond what the platform and the law already allow (section 6.4).
  • Not affiliated with Meta, Instagram, YouTube, Google, Reddit, OpenAI, Apple or any other company named in these terms (section 6.1).

5. How your saves reach Allkept

5.1 Instagram

You connect Instagram by sending a six-character code to @allkeptapp. That code links whichever Instagram account sent it to whichever Allkept account requested it — so do not give your code to anyone else, and do not send someone else's code. A code expires after ten minutes and can be used once. From then on, a post, reel or link you send to @allkeptapp becomes a save.

We only ever see the messages you send to us. We cannot see your feed, your followers, your other conversations, your stories or your Instagram saved collection, and we never post or message on your behalf. We reply in the thread you started, and only to confirm a save. You can stop those replies at any time by sending stop replies, or from Settings.

Some Instagram post shares arrive without the original link because Instagram does not include one. Those saves stay incomplete until you supply the link. That is a platform limitation, not a fault we can fix.

5.2 Links you paste or share

You can paste any link into the app, or share one into Allkept from another app. Our server — not your phone — fetches the public preview of the link (title, description, author, thumbnail) from the platform's public embed endpoint or from the page's public preview tags. The request identifies itself as AllkeptBot. If the page is private, behind a login or paywall, or refuses the request, the card simply has no preview.

5.3 A YouTube playlist

You give us the link to a public or unlisted playlist of your own. We read it periodically with Allkept's own YouTube API key and turn new videos in it into saves. We do not ask for, receive or store your Google or YouTube credentials, and we can see nothing on YouTube except that one playlist. YouTube shows Watch Later and Liked videos to nobody, including us. Disconnect the playlist at any time in Settings. Section 6.3 has the terms YouTube requires us to tell you about.

5.4 Reddit links

You can save a link to a Reddit post or comment. We store the link and read its public preview (the title and the poster's name) through Reddit's public embed endpoint. We do not log in to Reddit, do not use Reddit's Data API, and do not store Reddit posts beyond that preview. Some Reddit share links are resolved to the post they point at on your phone before being sent to us, because Reddit refuses to resolve them for servers.

5.5 Importing your older Instagram saves

You may upload the "saved posts" export that Meta gives you, so that your existing Instagram saves appear in Allkept. Upload only an export of your own account. We read the Instagram post links out of it and delete the file immediately afterwards.
